Selected Topics in Computer Science: Geospatial Data Science - 83992 - CSCI 4900 - 001
DEPARTMENT PERMISSION REQUIRED. Must attend first 2 classes or you may be dropped. Topic: Geospatial Data Science. This course will introduce fundamental concepts and techniques for working with geospatial data from a computational perspective. With the advancement of GPS and remote sensing technologies, enormous amounts of geospatial data are being collected from various domains—such as crime locations, detailed temporal road networks, earth observation imagery, and GPS trajectories. Geospatial data science applies computational methods to uncover interesting, previously unknown, and potentially useful patterns in large geo-referenced datasets. Such data holds the potential to transform our society by enabling environmentally friendly routing, precision agriculture, and global water resource management. Topics covered include: Introduction to Spatial Data Analysis Types of Spatial Data Foundational Spatial Statistics Spatial Colocation Discovery Spatial Outlier Analysis Spatial Prediction Hotspot Analysis Spatial Summarization Analysis of Spatial and Spatiotemporal Change Recent Trends in the Field Note: The specific topics may be adjusted by the instructor throughout the semester.

Selected Topics in Computer Science: Geospatial Data Science - 83993 - CSCI 6600 - 001
Must attend first 2 classes or you may be dropped. Topic: Geospatial Data Science. This course will introduce fundamental concepts and techniques for working with geospatial data from a computational perspective. With the advancement of GPS and remote sensing technologies, enormous amounts of geospatial data are being collected from various domains—such as crime locations, detailed temporal road networks, earth observation imagery, and GPS trajectories. Geospatial data science applies computational methods to uncover interesting, previously unknown, and potentially useful patterns in large geo-referenced datasets. Such data holds the potential to transform our society by enabling environmentally friendly routing, precision agriculture, and global water resource management. Topics covered include: Introduction to Spatial Data Analysis Types of Spatial Data Foundational Spatial Statistics Spatial Colocation Discovery Spatial Outlier Analysis Spatial Prediction Hotspot Analysis Spatial Summarization Analysis of Spatial and Spatiotemporal Change Recent Trends in the Field Note: The specific topics may be adjusted by the instructor throughout the semester.
